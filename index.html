Adaptive reverse sequence

Digits appear one at a time. Enter each sequence backward. Two trials are given at each span length; the test advances if at least one is correct.

Spatial grid recall

Watch cells light up, then reproduce the sequence by tapping cells in the same order.

Sustained attention

Tap the large symbol for every GO stimulus. Do not tap when the red octagon appears. A short practice block comes first.

Cognitive flexibility

Complete a number-only baseline first, then an alternating number-letter block. Tap targets in order; incorrect choices must be corrected.

Route reconstruction

Study a short route through the grid, then reproduce it after the route disappears.

Delayed word recall

Study the words for 15 seconds. They will disappear. After the delay, enter as many as you remember.
